# Application-Specific Tuning Profiles

Suspension tuning requires balancing competing priorities based on the vehicle's unique operating environment. Based on the data gathered, these principles scale to full-size applications as follows:

* **Motorcycles:** Benefit heavily from slightly overdamped rebound. This slows the extension of the shock, stabilizing tire contact during cornering and preventing abrupt chassis movements to enhance rider comfort and grip.
* **Off-Road / Trail:** Lightly underdamped setups allow for rapid suspension response to irregular, high-frequency obstacles, ensuring continuous ground contact.
* **Track / Racing:** Stiff spring rates paired with slight underdamping prioritize structural support under high cornering loads while maintaining high-speed compliance for precise mechanical grip over track imperfections.
* **Rally / Desert Racing:** Multi-stage springs paired with high damping control allow the vehicle to track highly uneven terrain while maintaining strict bottoming resistance during jump landings.
* **Luxury Vehicles:** Soft spring rates with slightly overdamped low-speed and underdamped high-speed characteristics maximize occupant isolation from harsh chassis inputs.
